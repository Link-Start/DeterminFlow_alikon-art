# Product

## Register

product

## Users

DeterminFlow serves developers and small teams who build, run, inspect, and distribute production AI workflows and reusable resources. They work in a dense desktop or browser application and expect state, ownership, recovery, and next actions to be obvious without reading internal implementation details.

## Product Purpose

DeterminFlow is a Workflow Runtime for turning probabilistic model behavior into workflows with explicit control flow, validation, recovery, and auditability. Its product interface should help users create and operate workflows, manage trusted local resources, and participate in the community marketplace without confusing local runtime state with website-hosted publishing state.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, capable. The product should feel like a mature developer tool: compact enough for daily work, clear under failure, and confident without decorative spectacle.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards that wrap every fact in an identical card.
- Interfaces that expose schemas, database or runtime terminology instead of user decisions.
- Decorative gradients, glass effects, oversized metrics, or motion that delays work.
- Marketplace pages that treat a submission receipt as the resource's complete management surface.
- Controls that imply immediate behavior when the underlying operation is staged, reviewed, or restart-dependent.

## Design Principles

1. Make the managed object and its lifecycle explicit.
2. Keep authoritative state and the next valid action adjacent.
3. Preserve failure reasons, recovery paths, and irreversible consequences at the decision point.
4. Prefer familiar developer-tool patterns and progressive disclosure over explanatory copy.
5. Never present planned or remote capabilities as locally completed facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, labels, and status announcements. Do not rely on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep primary workflows usable at narrow desktop and 390px mobile widths.
